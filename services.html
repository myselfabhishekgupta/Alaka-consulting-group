Capital Instruments

The Right Structure for Every Raise

Choosing the instrument matters as much as choosing the investor. We weigh dilution, cost of capital and control before recommending a structure.

Equity & Preference Shares

Common equity and compulsorily convertible preference shares (CCPS), with liquidation preferences, anti-dilution and board rights negotiated up front.

Convertible Notes & SAFEs

Documentation-light instruments for seed and bridge rounds, with valuation caps and discounts that reward early conviction.

Venture Debt

Non-dilutive term loans raised alongside equity to fund capex, lengthen runway or reach the next milestone.

Working Capital & Structured Credit

Receivable financing, revenue-based financing and mezzanine facilities matched to your cash-flow cycle.

Secondary Sales

Stake transfers, ESOP buybacks and partial exits that give early shareholders liquidity without a fresh primary issue.

Pre-IPO Placements

Private placements and anchor positioning for companies preparing to list on the main board or SME platform.

Engagement Scope

Clear Expectations From Day One

Every mandate begins with a written scope covering objectives, deliverables, timelines and responsibilities. Where an activity calls for a registered intermediary, legal counsel or another specialist, we say so up front and coordinate with them.

Typical deliverables

  • Capital requirement and funding-route assessment
  • Integrated financial model with scenario and sensitivity analysis
  • Valuation report with documented assumptions
  • Teaser, pitch deck and information memorandum
  • Investor long-list and outreach tracker
  • Term sheet comparison and negotiation notes
